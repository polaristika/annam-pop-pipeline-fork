# Importance of manure and fertilizer in gardens
Fruit plants require more nutrients than other crops because trees are permanently planted at one place for many years and they bear fruits and flowers as they grow continuously, hence, they should be supplied with manure, fertilizers and micro elements as per recommendations.

<a id='72bfca27-4353-401b-93f4-4d3228011d8b'></a>

Manure and fertilizer should be put in pots, mixed with soil and irrigated.For this, the tray should be dug before giving manure and fertilizer. # Importance of weeding and plowing in the care of fruit gardens

Weeding and plowing the land increases the fertility of the land.By breaking the hard layer of the soil, there will be better air circulation, the physical membership of the soil will improve, the activity of bacteria will increase, the ability to absorb water will increase and moisture will be maintained.Therefore, the garden should be plowed once or twice a year so that weeds will be controlled.

<a id='23b24dbe-7630-4356-a6eb-28b514a25246'></a>

Importance of irrigation in garden
To pay special attention to irrigation in gardens
What is needed is light but frequent watering of new plants.
Irrigation should be done in such a way that the root zone remains moist.

<a id='0ea6a6eb-b721-4532-8576-0b6e0d6c6928'></a>

Old plants that are still growing should be irrigated at regular intervals after the rainy season.Not enough irrigation also has an adverse effect on fruit production, because plants absorb nutrients in liquid state.Therefore, regularize the irrigation as per the requirement of the fruit trees and keep in mind that the irrigation water should not come in contact with the stem, that is, soil should be applied around the stem.

<a id='35a36ffd-aa52-4be9-8f24-5febd8add45e'></a>

* Guava-Irrigation- Do it at an interval of 7-10 days in summer and 15-20 days in winter.
* Mango- After the formation of fruits in mango, irrigate at an interval of 10-15 days.
* Lemon- Stop irrigation one month before the flowers bloom and irrigate as soon as the flowers bloom.
* Papaya- Irrigation at an interval of 7-10 days in summer and 15-20 days in winter.
* Amla- When new buds emerge in March, irrigate at an interval of 15 days till June.
* Pomegranate plants require constant moisture. Due to lack of moisture at the time of fruit formation, there is a possibility of the fruits bursting.

4. Cold storage –
Onions last for 8 to 10 months in an environment with low temperature (0-2 degree centigrade) and 65 to 70 percent humidity.During this period, there is a slight loss in weight while there is no outbreak of diseases.But soon after coming out of the cold storage, the problem of blooming occurs and this problem can be up to 40-50 percent in the first 15-20 days.Experiments conducted by Bhabha Atomic Energy Center in the past years revealed that the problem of eruption can be prevented by treatment with gamma radiation.Therefore, National Onion and Garlic Research Center in collaboration with Bhabha Atomic Energy Center conducted experiments on irradiation and cold storage.In which the irradiated onions were kept in normal environmental conditions and cold storage.Its results showed that irradiation can completely prevent the bloom that occurs after cold storage.The cost of storage in cold storage is high, but storing tubers for export and seed production will be beneficial.By jointly adopting cold storage and irradiation, storage losses can be greatly reduced (3-5 percent).For this, it is necessary that the radiation center and cold storage be built simultaneously.Presently radiation commercial facility is available in Lasalgaon (Maharashtra).

## 6. Maintenance of warehouses

The empty storehouse should be cleaned thoroughly and the remains of rotten bulbs, leaves, scales etc. of last season's onion should be collected and destroyed.There should not be any kind of residue left around the storehouse as it harbors disease causing germs and insects.Fungicides and insecticides should be sprayed.Storehouses should be repaired as per need.Wooden and iron strips or channels should be painted every 3–5 years.There is usually a problem of cracks in cement sheets.These cracks should be closed immediately, otherwise rain water may enter the storehouse and the stored onions may get spoiled.More infestation of rats has been seen in storehouses made of reeds or sugarcane leaves.Therefore, measures should be adopted to control rats.
