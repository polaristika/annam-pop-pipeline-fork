Comprehensive Ownership Practices:
* Farmers planting new pomegranate plantations should select and plant disease-free plants.
* If the plants are planted at a distance of 4×4 meters, the spread of the pest will be reduced.
* Scissors used for pruning should be dipped in detol / spirit / sodium hypochloride (1%) and used.
* Infected branches should be cut 2 inches below the infected part.Apply Bordeaux paste to the cut parts.
* Spray with 5% urea (50 grams per liter of water) or Ithral (2.0 to 2.5 ml per liter of water) before pruning.
