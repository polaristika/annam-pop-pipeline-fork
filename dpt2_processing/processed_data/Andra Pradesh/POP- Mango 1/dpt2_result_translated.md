Restoration of mango plantations:
Generally the lifespan of mango trees is more than 70 to 80 years.However, the yield of mango trees decreases after 30 to 40 years.The main reasons for this are drought, pests, drying of the tree bark, and bushy growth of trees.

<a id='d00d9278-a2de-43a9-9210-cae19ccbd582'></a>

option : [ ] In mango trees, any branches blocking sunlight and bushy branches (center opening) should be pruned.
option : [ ] Pruning of dry branches, pest-like and bushy branches of mango tree should be done.
option : [ ] Apply Bordeaux mixture to the cut branches.Pruned trees should be sprayed with (2 ml monocrotophos and 3 g copper oxychloride) in 1 liter of water.
option : [ ] Fertilizer (2 kg urea 6 kg single super phosphate and 2 kg murate of potash) should be applied at the base of pruned trees.
