Soils:
Gulaka, Tuva, Garapa and black soils that absorb water are suitable for turmeric cultivation.This crop does not tolerate waterlogging.Alluvial lands, salt and alkaline lands are not suitable for cultivation.

Mulching:
It is best to cover the ground with green leaf or dry leaves immediately after planting turmeric.Cover with green leaf or dry leaves like zeeluga, janumu, vempali, neem, dirasana, kanuga, gyricidia, wild leaf.This should not be mixed.The soil gets vigor and organic matter.

Pests:
There are three main pests that cause damage to the turmeric crop – tuber borer, leaf spot and leaf spot.

<a id='c5f7392c-f757-48a6-8bb3-bb9aabd585c7'></a>

Tuber rot:
Features:
* The plants in the field are dying and dying.
* The first dark leaves of plants fall off.
* Formation of water-soaked spots on the stem of the plant.
* If the plant is pulled, it will blow to the ground level.

<a id='31440391-44db-46ca-ad7c-723187c5984e'></a>

* Rotting of tubers, horns, and roots.

Prevention:
* Seed treatment must be done.
* Prescribed fertilizers should be applied on time.
* A kg of Trichoderma should be grown in 90 kg of cattle manure and 10 kg of neem flour for a week and spread in dukki per acre.
* Apply water based on rain forecast.
* As soon as the symptoms of the pest are observed, one gram of Ridomil Y.Z or 2 gms of Captan or 3 gms per liter of water is applied to the plant around it.Pour a solution mixed with copper oxychloride around the plant roots so that the soil is wet.

<a id='ed29f714-0257-48b9-95c7-cbf8cec268ef'></a>

Palm Blight (Blister Blight) :
Features:
* Oval, large spots, brown on the leaves are formed here and there from September.
Prevention:
* Seed treatment must be done.
* Dry leaves with rot should be cut and destroyed.Then one gram of carbonidism or 2.5 g per liter of water.Mancozeb should be sprayed in half ml of Apsa - 80 or soapy water like Sandovit 3-4 times at 15 days interval.

<a id='e16346f9-b856-4d54-80f3-886fae25936f'></a>

Leaf spot disease:
Features:
* Small brown spots are formed on the leaves.
Prevention:
* Seed treatment must be done.
* As soon as the pest is noticed, the palm should be sprayed with the same medicine prescribed for the pest twice in a period of 10 days.

<a id='3e8f2276-a3e8-447b-8806-2b4aced75d8b'></a>

Worms:
Beet fly (cavity) :
* The leaves near the sudi leaf wither and dry up.
* The leaves in the plant are easily blown.
* In a ripe beet, the pods of the fly's palate are white like a grain of rice.

<a id='a8a2f312-7948-4ae8-8622-ccc08ecba48d'></a>

Prevention:
* 2 ml per liter of water.Dimethoate or 1.6 ml.The sown horns should be cleaned and planted in a solution mixed with monocrotophos.
* 100 kg of neem powder per acre should be sprinkled evenly throughout the field as soon as the symptoms of tuber rot appear.
* If there is no neem seed, 10 kg of carbofuran pellets should be used per acre.Carbofuran pellets should be mixed with equal amount of sand and sprinkled in the field.

<a id='d41949bf-1a56-4610-ae9b-dc9816ac2406'></a>

Braided Wings:
2 ml per liter of water as soon as the insects are observed at the base of the leaves.
Dimethoate or 1.6 ml.Monocrotophos should be mixed and sprayed on the pear.

<a id='1e772f6c-2b20-42ff-8641-475be4401648'></a>

Iron deficiency:
Features:
* The center of the tender leaves turns white.If the defect is severe, the entire leaf will turn white.
Prevention:
* The soil should be dug occasionally to ensure that air is thrown to the separate zone.
* Ensure that water does not stand in the field.
* 5 grams of ferrous sulfate or 10 grams per liter of water when the symptoms appear.Annabedhi, one gram of lemon salt should be sprayed twice at 15 days interval.

<a id='0fa84ad5-9175-4602-ada9-e09785c3e859'></a>

Potassium Deficiency:
Features:
* Edges of leaves become pale and dry.
* Growth in plants is stunted.Fewer calls.
Prevention:
* Apply a comprehensive balanced fertilizer as per recommendation.
* 10g per liter of water when deficiency symptoms appear.Nutrients like Rajita 'K' or Multi 'K' should be sprayed twice at 15 days interval.
* Sewerage facility should be provided.
