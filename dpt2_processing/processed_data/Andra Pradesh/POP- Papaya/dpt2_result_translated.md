plant protection

1. Pinworms : It feeds on the roots of papaya from seedling stage to mature trees and stunts their growth.For prevention of these, 500 grams of Carbopuran pellets should be mixed with neem seeds for 100 bags of soil.

<a id='8f8d81ca-919f-484b-ac36-0beee9cbac6c'></a>

2. Sap rot: Plants rot at the sap stage
For prevention, 3 grams of dhiram per kg of seed is added to the seed
should be purified.Phytalon or Blytox 0.3% on plants
The solution should be sprayed.

<a id='d4bffbbd-b523-4efd-8254-9c04b10d25fd'></a>

3. Stem rot: This pest is more expected during rainy season.Therefore, the above-mentioned drugs should be sprayed first by providing a place for sewage to drain.

<a id='68f9d639-7590-49c3-8a00-6d4aab62a58c'></a>

4. Mosaic and leaf spot : This disease is spread by aphids.Plants affected by the pest stop growing, the leaves turn yellow, the stems bend down and finally the plants die.Affected plants should be identified and burned immediately.Dimethoate 0.2% or monocrotophos 0.175% should be sprayed to control its spread.
