Cultivation methods

<a id='4a8dadd3-6b76-4113-9d27-2c10a958fd9d'></a>

* Selection of seed pod in coconut and preparation method of narumadi :
Coconuts for seed should be collected only from selected mother trees of good quality.The mother trees in the plantation selected for seed should have high yield (average 100 pods/yr/tree) and pod quality (150 gms per pod).The age of the trees in the garden should be 15-40 years.Mother trees should be healthy and consistently high-yielding.Only fully developed pods (12 months old) on the tree should be collected for seed during April-May.The seed pods should be dried in the shade for 20 days and planted in Narumadi in the month of June.
* Production of Narumadi: Light loamy soils with good drainage are suitable for production of Narumadi.In rainy season, in water stagnant and heavy lands, high narumadi should be made.30 cm between rows in tall narumadi, 10-15 cm between two pods in a row.Planted at a distance, then water frequently so that moisture does not dry out.Avoid weeds.Insects and pests should be prevented with plant protection measures.Usually the seed germinates within 2 months of planting.Germination percentage is 65-70.
* Selection of coconut plants and planting method: To get good yield in coconut consistently, quality plants should be selected and planted.In Narumadi, plants with the characteristics of early sprouting, more leaves, early fatness (10 cm) and quick lengthening of leaves can be considered as quality plants.Plants with these characteristics should be selected.Plants should be healthy and free from any pests.1-1/2 సం.It is a good practice to plant old plants in the garden.Generally before the onset of monsoon i.e. in the months of April - May, silos should be removed and kept.The silos should be kept at a distance of 8 meters between the plant and the row (on any side).60 plants per acre can be planted.1 × 1 × 1 మీ.The size of the silos should be removed and allowed to dry well in summer.After removal in June - July, the pit should be filled with good soil / alluvial soil and well digested cattle manure (25 kg) + 500 gm super phosphate.The selected coconut plant should be placed in the center of the pit, filled with soil, the soil should be tamped down and watered immediately.

♦ Water Management: Moisture should be properly supplied to the coconut trees depending on the soil, nature and climate.In the delta areas, the practice of draining water into plantations is practiced.However, this method wastes a lot of irrigation water and pests can spread quickly.It is better to provide water by 'basin method' by placing a tray around the trees.By providing water to the gardens through drip method, water can be saved well.Water must be provided for 20 days in black soils and 10 days in light red soils.In light lands, it must be wetted every 5-7 days during summer months.Care should be taken in coconut plantations to avoid waterlogging.Due to water stress, the size of the pods and pods decreases.

* Characteristics and effect of waterlogging: On an average, a budding tree needs about 50-60 liters of irrigation water per day in the summer months.Farmers are not able to provide water to the coconut plantations as recommended by the ground water.As a result, coconut plantations are facing water stress.Due to this waterlogging, symptoms such as drooping of leaves in rows under dark trees in gardens, slight coating, and excessive dropping of fruit are seen.This waterlogging reduces the formation of new leaves.During the summer months when the daytime temperatures are high and the hot winds blow, the fertilization of flowers is reduced and the set of embryos is also greatly reduced.Also coconut yield, nut size and coconut quality also decrease.Therefore, in summer months, coconut husk or coconut leaf or coconut husk should be spread in the tree bed to store the moisture.

Key Nutrients in Coconut - Character

<a id='192e4cf9-97aa-444d-b2e0-eb4c6653f654'></a>

• Nitrogen: Nitrogen plays an important role in the growth of tender plants and early establishment of coconut.In the researches it has been shown that the fruit yield has increased by about 28 percent if the fruit trees are mixed with nitrogen and potash in the right milk.

• Phosphorus: Phosphorus is useful for the early growth of tender coconut plants to make them fat and strong, and for the formation of more leaves.Therefore, when the plants are planted in the field, adding 250 grams of phosphorus along with well-digested cattle dung to the soil, if the needle plant is planted, the plants will grow strongly.When the mature trees are mixed with phosphorus, nitrogen and potash fertilisers, the roots are well rooted and help to absorb nitrogen from the soil.

• Potash : Potassium is the most important macronutrient in coconut plantations.Due to this, the plants mature quickly.The number of copulations increases, the fertilization of the balls takes place properly and there is a chance to stand.The yield of coconut and oil in the pods is greatly increased.Potash makes plants resistant to pests and water stress.

• Details of fertilizers to be used in coconut plantations : From 1 year after planting, fertilizers should be used in recommended amounts.Application of organic fertilizers like neem flour, cattle manure, vermicompost and green manures are very beneficial.Yields are consistent.

* Fertilizer application method: When fertilizers are applied in the tree tray in a proper manner, they penetrate into the soil and allow the roots to absorb them.Fertilizers should be applied in two phases in June - July and September - October in two equal parts.A trench should be dug around the tree trunk at a distance of 3 feet, sprinkled with fertilizers, covered with soil and watered immediately.Salting the plants, cutting the roots etc. are not scientific practices.These actions can harm the trees.
* Inter-cultivation : The spaces between the coconut rows should be plowed cleanly twice a year i.e. in tholakari and the second phase after the rainy season.Thus weeds can be avoided.By cultivating several inter-crops in these areas, additional income can be generated.

• Role of Organic Fertilizers in Coconut: The coconut plant absorbs many important nutrients from the soil such as potassium, nitrogen, phosphorus, calcium and magnesium in high doses.Organic fertilizers are very useful in maintaining yield in coconut plantations.These fertilizers slowly decompose in the soil and release nutrients.Many types of organic fertilizers can be easily prepared by farmers in their own gardens with low investment.Research estimates that about 16 tons of organic matter (coconut leaves, husks, husks, etc.) produced from one hectare of coconut plantation is wasted.

1. Retains moisture especially in light soils.
2. Increases organic matter in the soil.
3. Increases the microbial population in the soil, thereby increasing the availability of nutrients to plant roots in the soil.

Biography:

<a id='b8100de5-7d14-4d8c-9eda-18c27638eba3'></a>

A sample of white ovoid glands 2 to 6 inches deep is placed individually in a moist area and covered with material.At the time of bursting, the buds become swollen and the color also turns yellow and brown.In seven to 20 days the embryos hatch and become larvae.A mother worm lays about 140 broods in 6, 7 stages.It nests in decaying organic matter, dead coconut trees, roots etc. in the garden.A woodworm that bursts from the gills has white, firm parts.Feeds heavily on organic matter and reaches dormancy in 99 to 180 days.The larvae turn red and brown, forming a smooth chamber to spend the hibernation stage.The mother worm emerges from the dormant stage in 17 to 62 days.It stays in the room for 10 days, then comes out and roams on coconut fronds or production centers of dead coconut trees or cattle manure piles, compost pits and lives for more than 200 days.Life cycle is completed in about 130 to 196 days.These insects are active throughout the year and cause damage to trees.

Prevention:
1. The fallen trees in the plantations should be removed and properly utilized or destroyed.
2. The stumps of the fallen trees (part of the ground) should be dug up and burnt.

Preparation of Organic Manure (Compost) with Coconut Husk:

Manufacturing of coconut fiber products plays a leading role in the coconut based industry.Coconut husk (coir pith) is the remaining dry material after the fiber or fiber is separated from coconut husks.Fiber is extracted from about 10,000 coconuts, and one ton of husk is obtained as a by-product.It means that approximately 90 grams of thread / linen and 180 grams of husk are produced from one coconut.This husk has high water storage capacity.Hence it can be used as a mulching material in light and sandy soils.However, coconut shell does not decompose quickly due to its high percentage of phenols, lignin and high carbon-nitrogen ratio.That is why the husk produced near the coconut extraction factories is useless and causes environmental pollution.There is a need to utilize this husk.Through the experiments carried out in various research laboratories, industrially this husk can be profitably used for the manufacture of briquettes, for the manufacture of sheets used for roofs of houses, for the manufacture of particle boards and agriculturally for the manufacture of compost.Coconut shell can be exported to Japan and Middle East countries.Composting coconut husks can detoxify the phenolics and lignin content, thereby promoting microbial growth.The size of the husk can be significantly reduced and nutrients in the soil that are unavailable to the plant can be converted into a form that is readily available.A method of converting coconut husk into fertilizer has recently been developed by the Central Plantation Crop Research Institute, Kasaragad.
