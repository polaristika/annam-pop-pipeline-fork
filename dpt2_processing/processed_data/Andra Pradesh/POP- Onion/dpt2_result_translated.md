Soils:
Well-drained and fertile red soils, clay soils,
Loamy soils are suitable.Onion could not tolerate the heat.
Suitable season for cultivation:
The months of June-July and September-October are suitable for soaking and planting.K.P.
For onion cultivation, June-August months are good for sowing.
Types:
Dark red varieties like Agrifound Dark Red, Bellarired, Agrifound Rose,
Bangalore Rose, Arka Kalyan, N-53 Suitable for kharif cultivation.Light red varieties
Agrifound Lightred, Nasik Red, Poosared, Arkaniketan suitable for rabi cultivation.

Water splashes:
Clotting stage and udder stage are moisture sensitive stages.During these stages water should be watered regularly to avoid waterlogging.Do not water for 10-15 days before harvesting.
Drying, Curing :

<a id='9d43fcf1-a80e-4adf-8dfd-bb8471ab9da0'></a>

During Kharif and Rabi seasons, the tubers should be dried in the field for 3-5 days.There is a special method called window curing for this.After removing the bulbs from the ground, the onion should be placed in rows and the leaves of the plant on the top of the bulbs in the next row should be kept covering the bulbs placed in the first row.Then dry in shade for 7-10 days.2-2.5 cm after shade drying.The tubers should be cut like a pitcher.

Plant protection:

Purple leaf spot:

<a id='0a1de43f-6f7a-45b8-b991-8e8bce6c0f72'></a>

For prevention Mancozeb (2.5 g/l) or Chlorothalonil (2 g/l) or Aliate (2 g/l) should be sprayed 2 times at 10-12 days interval.Adhesives such as Sandovit or Apsa-80 (0.5 ml/l) must be mixed and sprayed.

Eczema mites:

<a id='ffff5600-c408-4fc9-81cc-ed3484c1adf2'></a>

Avoid spraying with dimethoate (2 ml/l) or methyl dematan.

Rotting of tubers in storage:
Spraying carbondizum (1 g/l) at 90 days after planting can reduce the rotting of tubers in storage.
